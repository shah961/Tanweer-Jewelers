40 Years of Golden Legacy

Established in 1985 in Islampura, Lahore, Tanveer Jewellers has served generations with unyielding honesty and master craftsmanship.

The Art of Lahore Goldsmithing

Every piece crafted in our workshop carries the historic soul of Lahore's jewelry heritage. From traditional hand-carved Kundan chokers to lightweight contemporary daily wear, our master artisans meticulously melt, shape, stone-set, and polish each ornament to perfection.

Our Master Craft Process

1. Purity Melting

24K gold assayed and alloyed precisely for strength.

2. Hand Engraving

Intricate motif carving by senior Lahori ustads.

3. Gem Setting

Precision setting of precious stones and Kundan glass.

4. Hallmarking

Guaranteed certified karat purity testing.
